      How to play

      1. Same grid, two players. You and your opponent fill one arrowword together. Clues sit inside the shaded squares; the arrow shows where the answer goes.
      2. Tiles. Each turn starts with exactly 5 letter tiles in your hand, and every tile belongs somewhere in this grid. Tap a tile, then the square where you think it goes (or tap a square and type). Nothing is checked until you submit; tap a laid tile to take it back.
      3. Submit. Right tiles stay on the board for +1 each. Wrong tiles come back to your hand and cost −1 each.
      4. Words. For every word your submission completes you get +1, plus +1 for each letter in that word.
      5. Full hand. All 5 tiles right in one turn: +5.
      6. Turns. Submitting ends your turn. You can also pass or swap your whole hand. The turn timer (if one was chosen) submits whatever you laid out when it runs out.
      7. Winning. When the grid is full, the higher score wins.
