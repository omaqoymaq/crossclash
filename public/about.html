About CrossClash

CrossClash is a free, ad-free arrowword duel. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. When you play the bot, it is always shown as a bot.

Puzzles are made by CrossClash's own arrowword generator. Every clue is hand-written. Answers are drawn from common English words, ranked with wordfreq (CC BY-SA 4.0) and checked against WordNet 3.0 by Princeton University.
